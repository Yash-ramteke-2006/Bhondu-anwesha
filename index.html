I don't want you to ever feel like you have to become someone else to deserve love. You don't. You are already enough. If you need me, call me. I don't want you to feel like you have to hide parts of yourself from me. I want to know the real you. The happy you. The tired you. The annoyed you. The excited you. The emotional you. The completely random you. All of her. And I want to be someone with whom you can be completely yourself. Anwesha, thank you for entering my life. Thank you for the memories we've already made. Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every silly moment, every little bit of affection, and even the difficult moments that taught us something about each other.
